01 // WHOAMI

WHO AMI

I'm Arion, a student who lives between the terminal and the editor. I build apps that do one thing well and get out of your way. No accounts, no analytics, no permission requests I don't need.

My tools are offline-first, lightweight, and open source. I believe the best app is the one you forget is running. It just works, respects your privacy, and never asks for your data to function.

Currently deep in Jetpack Compose and modern Android architecture. Always learning. Always building.
